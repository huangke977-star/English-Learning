1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明学习计划、设定可执行目标、表达困难与调整，并用目标词汇完成一段学习反思。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
goaln. /ɡoʊl/set/achieve a goalI set a weekly learning goal.目标普通
focusn./v. /ˈfoʊkəs/focus on; stay focusedTry to focus on one skill.专注；重点普通
habitn. /ˈhæbɪt/build/break a habitReading daily builds a habit.习惯普通
improvev. /ɪmˈpruːv/improve a skill; improve atI want to improve my writing.改进；提高普通
reviewn./v. /rɪˈvjuː/review notes; a quick reviewReview your notes every Friday.复习；审查普通
practicen./v. /ˈpræktɪs/practice speaking; regular practiceRegular practice matters.练习普通
challengen./v. /ˈtʃælɪndʒ/face a challengeListening is still a challenge.挑战普通
strategyn. /ˈstrætədʒi/learning strategyThis strategy saves time.策略普通
achievev. /əˈtʃiːv/achieve a goal/resultShe achieved her goal.达成普通
give upv. phr. /ɡɪv ʌp/give up doing somethingDon't give up practicing.放弃日常

practice 在美式英语中名词和动词都拼作 practice。常说 practice speaking，不说 practice to speak。improve 可接宾语，也可用 improve at + 技能：improve at listening。

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
achieveachievement (n.)a sense of achievement 是成就感
improveimprovement (n.)make an improvement 比 make progress 更具体
strategystrategic (adj.)strategic plan 偏正式

goal 是可量化或明确的目标，plan 是达到目标的安排。写计划时优先说清频率、时长和完成条件，而不只说 I want to get better.

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I want improve my English.I want to improve my English.want 后接不定式
I practice to speak every day.I practice speaking every day.practice 后接动名词
I have a plan to my goal.I have a plan to achieve my goal.需要表达动作
I gave up to learn English.I gave up learning English.give up 后接动名词

5. 受控练习

练习 A：完成搭配

  1. set a weekly ___
  2. focus ___ pronunciation
  3. give up ___ excuses

参考答案：1 goal；2 on；3 making。

练习 B：改写为可执行目标

把 I want to improve English. 改为包含频率和具体技能的目标句。

参考答案示例：I will practice listening for 20 minutes five days a week.

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：说明未来 7 天的一项学习目标、两项行动和一个可能的困难。
  • 写作：写一段 90-110 词的学习计划，说明目标、策略、复习日期和如何衡量进步。
  • 自检：检查 want to、practice + -ing、give up + -ing 和每个目标是否可衡量。
