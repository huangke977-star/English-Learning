1. 测试目标

完成本单元后，你能够在限定时间内处理不同体裁，提取事实、推断语气、比较信息，并将阅读结果转成口头和书面输出。

2. 测试材料（阅读材料）

材料 A：课程通知

The Thursday conversation workshop will meet online this week because Room 2 is being repaired. The meeting will still begin at 6:30 p.m. Participants should open the link five minutes early and keep their microphones muted until the host calls on them.

材料 B：学习者留言

I can join from my phone, but I may arrive ten minutes late because of work. Could someone send me the discussion questions afterward? I will answer them before Friday.

4. 测试任务

任务 A：限时理解（建议 8 分钟）

  1. Why will the workshop be online?
  2. What time does it begin?
  3. What should participants do before the host calls on them?
  4. Why may the learner be late?
  5. What does the learner promise to do before Friday?

参考答案：1 Room 2 is being repaired. 2 6:30 p.m. 3 Open the link five minutes early and keep microphones muted. 4 Because of work. 5 Answer the discussion questions.

任务 B：整合与推断

  1. Is the learner unable to attend? Give evidence.
  2. What information is stated in A but not in B?

参考答案：1 No. The learner can join from a phone and only may be late. 2 The online reason, start time, early login instruction, and microphone rule.

任务 C：输出任务

  • 口头：用 45 秒向迟到的同学说明课程变更和注意事项。
  • 写作：写 90-110 词的回复，确认你能否参加、预计到达时间，并请求或提供讨论问题。

5. 输出任务与评分标准

维度达标表现需要改进
信息准确5 道事实题至少答对 4 道，时间和条件无误混淆原因、时间或行动
推断与比较能用证据回答 2 道整合题只抄句子，不说明证据
复述组织按原因、时间、规则、个人安排顺序表达信息顺序混乱
书面表达90-110 词，包含确认、时间和请求，语气合适信息缺失或语体不当
