2. 阅读材料：Why short breaks help learning

Short breaks can improve learning when they are planned. During a long study session, attention gradually decreases, especially when the task is difficult or repetitive. A five-minute break gives the brain a chance to recover before the next focused period. However, not every break has the same effect. Checking messages can make it harder to return to the task, while stretching, walking, or drinking water usually creates a clearer pause. One useful method is to study for 25 minutes, take a five-minute break, and then review what you learned. This method does not guarantee better results for everyone, but it gives learners a simple structure they can adjust. The most important point is to return to the task after the break rather than letting the break become the new activity.

结构拆解

首句提出主旨；第二至三句解释注意力和短休息的因果；第四句用对比说明不同休息效果；第五句给出方法；最后两句限制结论并重申行动。However、while、but 标出转折或限制。

4. 理解练习

练习 A：主旨与因果

  1. What is the main idea of the text?
  2. Why can a short break help?
  3. Why might checking messages be less useful than walking?
  4. What method does the text suggest?
  5. What limitation does the writer mention?

参考答案：1 Planned short breaks can support learning. 2 They let attention/brain recover. 3 Messages can make returning harder. 4 Study 25 minutes, break 5 minutes, then review. 5 It may not work equally well for everyone.

练习 B：证据判断

文中哪一句说明“休息不能变成新的主要活动”？

参考答案：The most important point is to return to the task after the break rather than letting the break become the new activity.

5. 输出任务

  • 阅读复述：用“主旨—原因—例子—限制”四句结构复述。
  • 写作迁移：写 120-150 词的学习方法说明，包含一个主张、两个理由和一个限制条件。
  • 自检：每个观点是否有原文证据；区分 effect（影响）和 affect（动词，影响）。
