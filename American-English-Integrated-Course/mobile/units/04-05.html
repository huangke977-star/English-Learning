1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明工作任务和进度，参加基础会议交流，并用清楚、专业而不过分正式的词汇提出跟进。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
taskn. /tæsk/complete/assign a taskI completed the first task.任务普通
deadlinen. /ˈdɛdˌlaɪn/meet/miss a deadlineThe deadline is Friday afternoon.截止时间普通
priorityn. /praɪˈɔrəti/top/high priorityThis is our top priority.优先事项普通
progressn. /ˈprɑɡrɛs/make/report progressWe have made good progress.进展普通
updaten./v. /ʌpˈdeɪt/provide an updateCould you provide an update?更新；进展说明普通
agendan. /əˈdʒɛndə/meeting agenda; add to the agendaI added it to the agenda.议程普通
follow upv. phr. /ˈfɑloʊ ʌp/follow up on/withI'll follow up with the client.跟进普通
coordinatev. /koʊˈɔrdəneɪt/coordinate with a teamWe need to coordinate with design.协调普通
approvev. /əˈpruːv/approve a requestThe manager approved the request.批准普通
postponev. /poʊˈspoʊn/postpone a meetingWe postponed the meeting until Monday.推迟普通

make progress 是固定搭配，通常不说 do progress。follow up on 接事项，follow up with 接人：follow up on the request，follow up with Sam。

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
approveapproval (n.), approved (adj.)get approval for 表示获得批准
coordinatecoordination (n.)名词常见于普通和正式书面语
postponepostponed (adj.)The meeting has been postponed. 常见于通知

对同事可说 Any updates?，简洁但适合熟悉的工作关系；在邮件或较正式场景用 Could you provide an update on ...?

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I have many works.I have a lot of work. 或 I have many tasks.work 通常不可数
We will discuss about it.We will discuss it.discuss 后不加 about
Please follow the client.Please follow up with the client.follow 与 follow up 含义不同
The meeting postponed.The meeting was postponed.被动句需要 be + 过去分词

5. 受控练习

练习 A：选择搭配

  1. We need to ___ the deadline. (meet / do)
  2. Please ___ an update by noon. (provide / make)
  3. I will follow up ___ the request tomorrow. (on / with)

参考答案：1 meet；2 provide；3 on。

练习 B：语体改写

把 Any updates? 改为适合工作邮件的句子。

参考答案：Could you provide an update on this task?

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：做一次 45 秒工作进度汇报，说明任务、进展、风险和下一步。
  • 写作：写一封 90-120 词的跟进邮件，包含议程或任务、截止时间、需要对方确认的内容和礼貌结尾。
  • 自检：检查 work/tasks、discuss、follow up on/with 和被动通知结构。
