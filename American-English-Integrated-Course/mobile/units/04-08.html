1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够描述设备和网络问题，说明基本操作，并用清楚的词汇向客服或同事求助。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
devicen. /dɪˈvaɪs/mobile device; connect a deviceThis device needs an update.设备普通
screenn. /skrin/screen is frozen/crackedMy screen is frozen.屏幕普通
chargern. /ˈtʃɑrdʒɚ/plug in a chargerI forgot my phone charger.充电器日常
batteryn. /ˈbætəri/charge/check the batteryThe battery is almost dead.电池日常
passwordn. /ˈpæsˌwɝd/reset/enter a passwordI need to reset my password.密码普通
accountn. /əˈkaʊnt/create/access an accountI can't access my account.账户普通
log inv. phr. /lɔɡ ɪn/log in to a sitePlease log in to the portal.登录普通
uploadv. /ʌpˈloʊd/upload a file/photoUpload the file before noon.上传普通
downloadv. /ˈdaʊnˌloʊd/download an app/fileI downloaded the latest version.下载普通
updaten./v. /ʌpˈdeɪt/install an updateThe app needs an update.更新普通

log in 表示登录，sign up 表示注册；log in to 后接网站或账户。说明技术问题时按“设备 + 现象 + 已尝试步骤”组织：My laptop won't connect to Wi-Fi. I restarted it, but the problem remains.

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
connectconnection (n.), connected (adj.)a stable connection
accessaccessible (adj.)access an account 不加 to 作动词
securesecurity (n.), securely (adv.)账户安全说明中常见

The battery is dead 是日常说法，意思是电量耗尽；The device is powered off 更中性、偏书面。不要在公开消息中发送真实密码或验证码。

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I can't login my account.I can't log in to my account.动词短语分写并接 to
Please access to the page.Please access the page.access 作动词不接 to
I uploaded to the file.I uploaded the file.upload 直接接宾语
The Wi-Fi doesn't connect.My laptop won't connect to Wi-Fi.说明设备连接更自然

5. 受控练习

练习 A：选择短语

  1. I can't ___ to the portal. (log in / sign up)
  2. Please ___ the form as a PDF. (upload / download)
  3. I need to ___ my password. (reset / restart)

参考答案：1 log in；2 upload；3 reset。

练习 B：问题报告

用 screen / restarted / still 完成：My ___ is frozen. I ___ the device, but it is ___ frozen.

参考答案：screen / restarted / still。

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：向客服说明一个设备问题，包含设备、现象、时间和已尝试的解决方法。
  • 写作：写一条 80-100 词的技术支持工单，不写真实密码，提出一个明确请求。
  • 自检：检查 log in to、access、upload/download 和问题描述顺序。
