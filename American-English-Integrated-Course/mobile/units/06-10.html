06-10
新闻和观点文章

完成本单元后，你能够区分新闻报道中的事实、引语和评论，识别观点文章的立场、理由和不确定性。

状态：已完成（审查与复审通过） 所属册：Book6 难度：中级 内容类型：分级阅读与观点辨析

1. 阅读目标

完成本单元后，你能够区分新闻报道中的事实、引语和评论，识别观点文章的立场、理由和不确定性。

2. 阅读材料

City opens two new bus lanes The city opened two bus-only lanes downtown on Monday. According to the transportation department, the lanes are expected to reduce travel times during the morning rush. Some shop owners said they were concerned about losing parking spaces, while the transit workers' union welcomed the change. The city will collect traffic data for three months before deciding whether to expand the program. In an opinion column, local writer Maya Chen argued that the project should continue, but she also called for more delivery zones near small businesses.

结构拆解

标题和第一句提供新闻事实；According to... 标出来源；said 引出不同群体观点；will collect...before deciding 是后续计划；最后一句明确“观点专栏”及作者立场，同时保留让步建议。报道中的 expected 不代表已证实结果。

3. 核心词汇

词语词性与 IPA语境义搭配或提示
lanen. /leɪn/车道bus-only lane
rushn. /rʌʃ/高峰时段morning rush
departmentn. /dɪˈpɑrtmənt/部门transportation department
concernedadj. /kənˈsɝnd/担忧的concerned about
expandv. /ɪkˈspænd/扩大expand a program
arguev. /ˈɑrɡju/论证、主张argue that

4. 理解练习

练习 A：事实、引语与观点

  1. What did the city open?
  2. What result is expected?
  3. Which groups expressed different reactions?
  4. How long will the city collect data?
  5. What is Maya Chen's position, and what additional idea does she offer?

参考答案：1 Two bus-only lanes downtown. 2 Shorter morning travel times. 3 Shop owners and the transit workers' union. 4 Three months. 5 She supports continuing the project and suggests more delivery zones.

练习 B：确定性判断

The lanes reduce travel times. 与 The lanes are expected to reduce travel times. 哪句更确定？为什么？

参考答案：第一句更确定，直接陈述结果；第二句明确是预期，尚未由三个月数据证实。

5. 输出任务

  • 阅读复述：用“已知事实—不同反应—后续计划—作者观点”复述。
  • 写作迁移：写 130-160 词的短观点文，先陈述一个公共问题，再给出立场、证据和一个让步。
  • 自检：给事实、引语和观点分别做标记，不把 according to 后的信息当作作者亲自证实的事实。
