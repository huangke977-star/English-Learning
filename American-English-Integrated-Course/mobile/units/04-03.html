1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够点餐、询问商品信息、完成付款，并使用准确的数量、价格和退换货表达。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
ordern./v. /ˈɔrdɚ/place/take an orderI'd like to place an order.点单；订单日常
ingredientn. /ɪnˈɡridiənt/main/fresh ingredientsWhat ingredients are in this soup?食材普通
portionn. /ˈpɔrʃən/a large/small portionThe portion is large enough to share.一份的量普通
receiptn. /rɪˈsiːt/keep/ask for a receiptCould I have a receipt, please?收据日常
pricen. /praɪs/reasonable price; price rangeThe price is reasonable.价格普通
affordv. /əˈfɔrd/can/can't afford toI can't afford to buy it right now.负担得起普通
discountn./v. /ˈdɪskaʊnt/get/offer a discountIs there a student discount?折扣日常
exchangen./v. /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/exchange an item forCan I exchange this for a larger size?换货；交换普通
refundn./v. /ˈriːfʌnd/request/get a refundI'd like to request a refund.退款普通

price 是价格本身，cost 是成本或“花费”：The meal costs $15.。afford 后接名词或 to + 动词，不说 afford buying。

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
orderorder (v./n.), ordered (adj.)place an order 比 make an order 更常用
discountdiscounted (adj.)discounted price 表示折后价
refundrefundable (adj.)refundable ticket 常见于旅行和服务条款

点餐可说 Can I get...?，适合非正式到普通服务场景；在较正式服务场景可用 I'd like...。投诉或退款时保持简洁、说明事实，不需使用攻击性表达。

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I want order food.I want to order food.want 后接 to + 动词
How much is it cost?How much does it cost?不能同时用 is 和 cost
I can't afford it to buy.I can't afford to buy it.afford to + 动词
We are out milk.We are out of milk.固定搭配 out of

5. 受控练习

练习 A：选择正确搭配

  1. I'd like to ___ an order. (place / do)
  2. Can I ___ this shirt for another size? (exchange / return)
  3. We are ___ eggs. (running out of / running out)

参考答案：1 place；2 exchange；3 running out of。

练习 B：情境改写

把直接句 Give me my money back. 改成礼貌的退款请求。

参考答案：I'd like to request a refund, please. 或 Could I get a refund for this item?

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：完成一段点餐或退换货角色扮演，询问价格、尺寸或食材，并作出一个请求。
  • 写作：写一封 80-100 词的退款或换货消息，写明商品、问题、购买时间和你希望的解决方式。
  • 自检：检查数量词、cost 问句、afford to 和礼貌请求。
