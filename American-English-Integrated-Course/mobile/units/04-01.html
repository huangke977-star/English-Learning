人物、家庭和日常生活

1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够介绍人物和家庭关系，描述基本日常安排，并用常见搭配而不是逐词翻译表达生活细节。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
relativen. /ˈrɛlətɪv/close relativeMy close relatives live nearby.亲属普通
roommaten. /ˈrumˌmeɪt/share an apartment withI share an apartment with a roommate.室友日常
neighborn. /ˈneɪbɚ/a friendly neighborOur neighbor is very friendly.邻居日常
routinen. /ruˈtin/daily/morning routineMy morning routine starts at seven.日常惯例普通
householdn. /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld/household choresWe divide the household chores.家庭；家务普通
choren. /tʃɔr/do the choresI do the chores on Saturday.琐碎家务日常
preparev. /prɪˈpɛr/prepare breakfast/forShe prepares breakfast for the kids.准备普通
take care ofv. phr. /teɪk kɛr əv/take care of a childHe takes care of his grandfather.照顾日常
get along withv. phr. /ɡɛt əˈlɔŋ wɪð/get along well withI get along well with my coworkers.相处融洽日常
independentadj. /ˌɪndɪˈpɛndənt/become independentShe became more independent.独立的普通

do chores 是常用搭配，不说 make chores。take care of 后接人、动物或事物；在日常口语中可说 take care of the kids，正式写作也自然。

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
relativerelated (adj.)related to 表示“与……有关”，不是“亲戚”
preparepreparation (n.), prepared (adj.)prepare for an exam；prepare a meal
independentindependence (n.), independently (adv.)live independently 常用于普通或正式表达

口语中可说 My folks live nearby.（我的父母/家人住得近），但它较随意；不应用于正式介绍家庭成员的邮件。

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I make housework.I do housework. 或 I do the chores.英语搭配用 do
He looks after to his child.He looks after his child.look after 后不加 to
My family has four people.There are four people in my family.后者更自然
I live with my parent.（多位）I live with my parents.两位父母用复数

5. 受控练习

练习 A：选择搭配

  1. I ___ the chores after dinner. (do / make)
  2. She ___ her younger brother. (takes care of / takes care)
  3. We ___ well with our neighbors. (get along / get along with)

参考答案：1 do；2 takes care of；3 get along。

练习 B：词形填空

  1. Please ___ for the meeting. (prepare)
  2. Living ___ is an important skill. (independent)

参考答案：1 prepare；2 independently。

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：用 45 秒介绍你的家庭或同住的人，至少使用 5 个本章搭配。
  • 写作：写 70-90 词的“我的一天”，包含一个家务、一个照顾行为和一个人与人相处的细节。
  • 自检：圈出所有动词，确认 do/make、take care of 和 get along with 搭配正确。
