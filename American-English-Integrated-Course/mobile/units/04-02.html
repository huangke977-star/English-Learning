1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明时间、地点和出行路线，正确使用常见移动动词和时间表达。

2. 核心词汇与搭配

词语词性与 IPA常用搭配例句中文语体
schedulen./v. /ˈskɛdʒuːl/busy schedule; schedule a meetingMy schedule is busy this week.日程；安排普通
appointmentn. /əˈpɔɪntmənt/make/have an appointmentI have a doctor's appointment at ten.预约普通
delayn./v. /dɪˈleɪ/be delayed; traffic delayThe train was delayed by 20 minutes.延误普通
routen. /ruːt/ 或 /raʊt/take a route; fastest routeThis is the fastest route downtown.路线普通
directionn. /dəˈrɛkʃən/ask for/give directionsCould you give me directions?方向；路线说明日常
arrivev. /əˈraɪv/arrive at/in; arrive on timeWe arrived at the station on time.到达普通
leavev. /liːv/leave for; leave fromThe bus leaves for Boston at six.离开；出发普通
pick upv. phr. /pɪk ʌp/pick someone upCan you pick me up at the airport?接某人；拿起日常
drop offv. phr. /drɑp ɔf/drop someone offI'll drop you off at the corner.送某人到某处日常
nearbyadv./adj. /ˌnɪrˈbaɪ/live nearby; a nearby storeIs there a nearby pharmacy?附近普通

arrive at 常用于较小地点（the station），arrive in 常用于城市和国家（Chicago）。get to 更口语：What time did you get to work?

route 在美式英语中两种读法都常见；学习时任选一种保持稳定，听力中要能识别另一种。

3. 词形与语体

基本词常见变化使用提醒
delaydelayed (adj.), delay (v.)The flight was delayed.
directiondirect (v./adj.), directly (adv.)go directly home 表示直接回家
appointmentappoint (v.)日常学习中优先掌握名词和搭配

pick up 和 drop off 可分开：pick me up，也可接名词：pick up the package。代词放在短语中间，不说 pick up me。

4. 常见错误

错误修正原因
I arrived to the airport.I arrived at the airport.arrive 不接 to
The bus will leave to Boston.The bus will leave for Boston.目的地前常用 leave for
Please pick up me.Please pick me up.代词置于短语动词中间
I am late 20 minutes.I am 20 minutes late.late 前放时长

5. 受控练习

练习 A：介词和短语动词

  1. We arrived ___ the hotel at noon. (at / to)
  2. She is leaving ___ New York tomorrow. (for / to)
  3. Can you ___ me ___ after work? (pick / up)

参考答案：1 at；2 for；3 pick ... up。

练习 B：路线表达

把两句合并：The subway is delayed. We will take a bus.

参考答案：The subway is delayed, so we will take a bus.

6. 输出任务

  • 口语：说明你从家到一个常去地点的路线，包含出发时间、交通方式和至少一个方向词。
  • 写作：给朋友写一条 50-70 词的消息，说明你会迟到、原因、预计到达时间和替代安排。
  • 自检：检查 arrive at/in、leave for 和可分短语动词中的代词位置。
