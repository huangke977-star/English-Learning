# 06-11 跨材料比较阅读

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book6  
> 难度：中级  
> 内容类型：分级阅读与信息整合

## 1. 阅读目标

完成本单元后，你能够比较两份材料的共同点、差异、证据和语气，并写出有依据的综合结论。

## 2. 阅读材料

### 材料 A：社区图书馆通知

> The library will extend its weekday hours from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. beginning June 1. The change is a six-month trial. The library asks visitors to complete a short survey so staff can evaluate the program.

### 材料 B：读者协会评论

> Longer library hours could help people who work full time, especially those who cannot visit during the afternoon. However, the library should publish monthly attendance numbers before making the change permanent. Extra hours are useful only if enough residents use them to justify the cost.

### 比较框架

- 材料 A 是机构通知，重点是事实、日期和行动要求。
- 材料 B 是观点评论，重点是受益人、条件和成本问题。
- 两者都讨论延长开放时间，但 B 额外提出“使用量是否值得成本”的判断标准。

## 4. 理解练习

### 练习 A：比较信息

1. What change does the library announce?
2. How long is the trial?
3. What action does the library ask visitors to take?
4. Who might benefit, according to Material B?
5. What evidence does Material B request before a permanent change?

参考答案：1 Weekday hours will run 8 a.m.-8 p.m. 2 Six months. 3 Complete a survey. 4 People who work full time or cannot visit in the afternoon. 5 Monthly attendance numbers.

### 练习 B：综合结论

用 `Both texts...`、`However...`、`Therefore...` 写三句综合结论。

参考答案示例：`Both texts support considering longer hours. However, the review asks for evidence about use and cost. Therefore, the trial and survey data should be evaluated before a permanent decision.`

## 5. 输出任务

- 阅读复述：先分别用两句复述 A、B，再用两句比较共同点和差异。
- 写作迁移：写 140-170 词的比较短文，包含一个共同点、两个差异、一个证据和结论。
- 自检：每个结论都能指回某份材料；区分“材料说了什么”和“我推断什么”。
