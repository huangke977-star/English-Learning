# 04-01 人物、家庭和日常生活

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）

> 难度：基础

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够介绍人物和家庭关系，描述基本日常安排，并用常见搭配而不是逐词翻译表达生活细节。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| relative | n. `/ˈrɛlətɪv/` | close relative | My close relatives live nearby. | 亲属 | 普通 |
| roommate | n. `/ˈrumˌmeɪt/` | share an apartment with | I share an apartment with a roommate. | 室友 | 日常 |
| neighbor | n. `/ˈneɪbɚ/` | a friendly neighbor | Our neighbor is very friendly. | 邻居 | 日常 |
| routine | n. `/ruˈtin/` | daily/morning routine | My morning routine starts at seven. | 日常惯例 | 普通 |
| household | n. `/ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld/` | household chores | We divide the household chores. | 家庭；家务 | 普通 |
| chore | n. `/tʃɔr/` | do the chores | I do the chores on Saturday. | 琐碎家务 | 日常 |
| prepare | v. `/prɪˈpɛr/` | prepare breakfast/for | She prepares breakfast for the kids. | 准备 | 普通 |
| take care of | v. phr. `/teɪk kɛr əv/` | take care of a child | He takes care of his grandfather. | 照顾 | 日常 |
| get along with | v. phr. `/ɡɛt əˈlɔŋ wɪð/` | get along well with | I get along well with my coworkers. | 相处融洽 | 日常 |
| independent | adj. `/ˌɪndɪˈpɛndənt/` | become independent | She became more independent. | 独立的 | 普通 |

`do chores` 是常用搭配，不说 `make chores`。`take care of` 后接人、动物或事物；在日常口语中可说 `take care of the kids`，正式写作也自然。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| relative | related (adj.) | `related to` 表示“与……有关”，不是“亲戚” |
| prepare | preparation (n.), prepared (adj.) | `prepare for an exam`；`prepare a meal` |
| independent | independence (n.), independently (adv.) | `live independently` 常用于普通或正式表达 |

口语中可说 `My folks live nearby.`（我的父母/家人住得近），但它较随意；不应用于正式介绍家庭成员的邮件。

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I make housework.` | `I do housework.` 或 `I do the chores.` | 英语搭配用 `do` |
| `He looks after to his child.` | `He looks after his child.` | `look after` 后不加 `to` |
| `My family has four people.` | `There are four people in my family.` | 后者更自然 |
| `I live with my parent.`（多位） | `I live with my parents.` | 两位父母用复数 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择搭配

1. I ___ the chores after dinner. (`do / make`)
2. She ___ her younger brother. (`takes care of / takes care`)
3. We ___ well with our neighbors. (`get along / get along with`)

参考答案：1 `do`；2 `takes care of`；3 `get along`。

### 练习 B：词形填空

1. Please ___ for the meeting. (`prepare`)
2. Living ___ is an important skill. (`independent`)

参考答案：1 `prepare`；2 `independently`。

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：用 45 秒介绍你的家庭或同住的人，至少使用 5 个本章搭配。
- 写作：写 70-90 词的“我的一天”，包含一个家务、一个照顾行为和一个人与人相处的细节。
- 自检：圈出所有动词，确认 `do/make`、`take care of` 和 `get along with` 搭配正确。
