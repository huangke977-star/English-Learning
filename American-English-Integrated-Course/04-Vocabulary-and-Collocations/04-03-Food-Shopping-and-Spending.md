# 04-03 食物、购物和消费

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book4  
> 难度：基础  
> 内容类型：主题词汇、搭配与输出

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够点餐、询问商品信息、完成付款，并使用准确的数量、价格和退换货表达。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| order | n./v. `/ˈɔrdɚ/` | place/take an order | I'd like to place an order. | 点单；订单 | 日常 |
| ingredient | n. `/ɪnˈɡridiənt/` | main/fresh ingredients | What ingredients are in this soup? | 食材 | 普通 |
| portion | n. `/ˈpɔrʃən/` | a large/small portion | The portion is large enough to share. | 一份的量 | 普通 |
| receipt | n. `/rɪˈsiːt/` | keep/ask for a receipt | Could I have a receipt, please? | 收据 | 日常 |
| price | n. `/praɪs/` | reasonable price; price range | The price is reasonable. | 价格 | 普通 |
| afford | v. `/əˈfɔrd/` | can/can't afford to | I can't afford to buy it right now. | 负担得起 | 普通 |
| discount | n./v. `/ˈdɪskaʊnt/` | get/offer a discount | Is there a student discount? | 折扣 | 日常 |
| exchange | n./v. `/ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/` | exchange an item for | Can I exchange this for a larger size? | 换货；交换 | 普通 |
| refund | n./v. `/ˈriːfʌnd/` | request/get a refund | I'd like to request a refund. | 退款 | 普通 |
| run out of | v. phr. `/rʌn aʊt əv/` | run out of milk | We're running out of milk. | 用完 | 日常 |

`price` 是价格本身，`cost` 是成本或“花费”：`The meal costs $15.`。`afford` 后接名词或 `to + 动词`，不说 `afford buying`。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| order | order (v./n.), ordered (adj.) | `place an order` 比 `make an order` 更常用 |
| discount | discounted (adj.) | `discounted price` 表示折后价 |
| refund | refundable (adj.) | `refundable ticket` 常见于旅行和服务条款 |

点餐可说 `Can I get...?`，适合非正式到普通服务场景；在较正式服务场景可用 `I'd like...`。投诉或退款时保持简洁、说明事实，不需使用攻击性表达。

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I want order food.` | `I want to order food.` | `want` 后接 `to + 动词` |
| `How much is it cost?` | `How much does it cost?` | 不能同时用 `is` 和 `cost` |
| `I can't afford it to buy.` | `I can't afford to buy it.` | `afford to + 动词` |
| `We are out milk.` | `We are out of milk.` | 固定搭配 `out of` |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择正确搭配

1. I'd like to ___ an order. (`place / do`)
2. Can I ___ this shirt for another size? (`exchange / return`)
3. We are ___ eggs. (`running out of / running out`)

参考答案：1 `place`；2 `exchange`；3 `running out of`。

### 练习 B：情境改写

把直接句 `Give me my money back.` 改成礼貌的退款请求。

参考答案：`I'd like to request a refund, please.` 或 `Could I get a refund for this item?`

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：完成一段点餐或退换货角色扮演，询问价格、尺寸或食材，并作出一个请求。
- 写作：写一封 80-100 词的退款或换货消息，写明商品、问题、购买时间和你希望的解决方式。
- 自检：检查数量词、`cost` 问句、`afford to` 和礼貌请求。
