# 04-07 情绪、关系和社交

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book4  
> 难度：初级  
> 内容类型：主题词汇、搭配与输出

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够描述情绪和人际关系，表达支持、失望、感谢或边界，并在社交场景中保持自然语气。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| excited | adj. `/ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/` | be excited about | I'm excited about the trip. | 兴奋的 | 日常 |
| nervous | adj. `/ˈnɝvəs/` | feel nervous about | She feels nervous about the interview. | 紧张的 | 普通 |
| disappointed | adj. `/ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntɪd/` | be disappointed with/by | We were disappointed by the result. | 失望的 | 普通 |
| grateful | adj. `/ˈɡreɪtfəl/` | be grateful for | I'm grateful for your help. | 感激的 | 普通 |
| support | n./v. `/səˈpɔrt/` | offer/get support | Thank you for your support. | 支持 | 普通 |
| trust | n./v. `/trʌst/` | build/earn trust | Trust takes time to build. | 信任 | 普通 |
| boundary | n. `/ˈbaʊndəri/` | set/respect a boundary | It's okay to set a boundary. | 界限 | 普通 |
| invite | v. `/ɪnˈvaɪt/` | invite someone to | I invited her to dinner. | 邀请 | 日常 |
| decline | v. `/dɪˈklaɪn/` | politely decline | He politely declined the invitation. | 谢绝 | 普通 |
| get together | v. phr. `/ɡɛt təˈɡɛðɚ/` | get together with friends | Let's get together this weekend. | 聚会 | 日常 |

情绪形容词要区分“让人产生情绪”和“人的感受”：`The news was surprising.`（消息令人惊讶），`I was surprised.`（我感到惊讶）。`grateful for` 后接帮助或事物，`grateful to` 后接人：`grateful to you for your help`。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| disappoint | disappointment (n.), disappointing (adj.) | 事物常用 `-ing`，人的感受用 `-ed` |
| support | supportive (adj.) | `a supportive friend` |
| invite | invitation (n.) | `accept/decline an invitation` |

拒绝邀请时，`I can't make it` 比 `I refuse` 更自然、更缓和。`No worries` 是日常口语，正式邮件可用 `Thank you for understanding.`

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I am exciting about it.` | `I am excited about it.` | 人的感受用 `-ed` |
| `She is disappointed on me.` | `She is disappointed in me.` | 对人常用 `disappointed in` |
| `I am grateful to your help.` | `I am grateful for your help.` | `for` 接事物，`to` 接人 |
| `I invite him for dinner.` | `I invited him to dinner.` | 固定搭配 `invite someone to` |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择形式

1. The movie was (boring / bored).
2. I felt (boring / bored) during the movie.
3. Thank you. I'm grateful (for / to) your advice.

参考答案：1 `boring`；2 `bored`；3 `for`。

### 练习 B：礼貌改写

把 `I don't want to go.` 改成自然的拒绝邀请。

参考答案示例：`Thanks for inviting me, but I can't make it this time.`

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：模拟接受或婉拒邀请，并说明一个简单原因。
- 写作：写 80-100 词的社交消息，表达感谢、描述情绪、提出一次邀请或设置一个合理边界。
- 自检：检查 `-ed/-ing`、`grateful for/to` 和邀请、拒绝的语气。
