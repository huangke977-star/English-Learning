# 04-02 时间、地点和移动

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book4  
> 难度：基础  
> 内容类型：主题词汇、搭配与输出

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明时间、地点和出行路线，正确使用常见移动动词和时间表达。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| schedule | n./v. `/ˈskɛdʒuːl/` | busy schedule; schedule a meeting | My schedule is busy this week. | 日程；安排 | 普通 |
| appointment | n. `/əˈpɔɪntmənt/` | make/have an appointment | I have a doctor's appointment at ten. | 预约 | 普通 |
| delay | n./v. `/dɪˈleɪ/` | be delayed; traffic delay | The train was delayed by 20 minutes. | 延误 | 普通 |
| route | n. `/ruːt/` 或 `/raʊt/` | take a route; fastest route | This is the fastest route downtown. | 路线 | 普通 |
| direction | n. `/dəˈrɛkʃən/` | ask for/give directions | Could you give me directions? | 方向；路线说明 | 日常 |
| arrive | v. `/əˈraɪv/` | arrive at/in; arrive on time | We arrived at the station on time. | 到达 | 普通 |
| leave | v. `/liːv/` | leave for; leave from | The bus leaves for Boston at six. | 离开；出发 | 普通 |
| pick up | v. phr. `/pɪk ʌp/` | pick someone up | Can you pick me up at the airport? | 接某人；拿起 | 日常 |
| drop off | v. phr. `/drɑp ɔf/` | drop someone off | I'll drop you off at the corner. | 送某人到某处 | 日常 |
| nearby | adv./adj. `/ˌnɪrˈbaɪ/` | live nearby; a nearby store | Is there a nearby pharmacy? | 附近 | 普通 |

`arrive at` 常用于较小地点（`the station`），`arrive in` 常用于城市和国家（`Chicago`）。`get to` 更口语：`What time did you get to work?`

`route` 在美式英语中两种读法都常见；学习时任选一种保持稳定，听力中要能识别另一种。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| delay | delayed (adj.), delay (v.) | `The flight was delayed.` |
| direction | direct (v./adj.), directly (adv.) | `go directly home` 表示直接回家 |
| appointment | appoint (v.) | 日常学习中优先掌握名词和搭配 |

`pick up` 和 `drop off` 可分开：`pick me up`，也可接名词：`pick up the package`。代词放在短语中间，不说 `pick up me`。

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I arrived to the airport.` | `I arrived at the airport.` | `arrive` 不接 `to` |
| `The bus will leave to Boston.` | `The bus will leave for Boston.` | 目的地前常用 `leave for` |
| `Please pick up me.` | `Please pick me up.` | 代词置于短语动词中间 |
| `I am late 20 minutes.` | `I am 20 minutes late.` | `late` 前放时长 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：介词和短语动词

1. We arrived ___ the hotel at noon. (`at / to`)
2. She is leaving ___ New York tomorrow. (`for / to`)
3. Can you ___ me ___ after work? (`pick / up`)

参考答案：1 `at`；2 `for`；3 `pick ... up`。

### 练习 B：路线表达

把两句合并：`The subway is delayed. We will take a bus.`

参考答案：`The subway is delayed, so we will take a bus.`

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：说明你从家到一个常去地点的路线，包含出发时间、交通方式和至少一个方向词。
- 写作：给朋友写一条 50-70 词的消息，说明你会迟到、原因、预计到达时间和替代安排。
- 自检：检查 `arrive at/in`、`leave for` 和可分短语动词中的代词位置。
