# 04-04 健康、身体和求助

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够描述常见不适、说明症状持续时间、理解基本建议，并在需要时清楚求助。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| symptom | n. `/ˈsɪmptəm/` | have/describe symptoms | What symptoms do you have? | 症状 | 普通 |
| pain | n. `/peɪn/` | sharp/dull pain; be in pain | I have a sharp pain in my back. | 疼痛 | 普通 |
| sore | adj. `/sɔr/` | sore throat/muscles | I have a sore throat. | 疼痛的；发炎的 | 日常 |
| ache | n./v. `/eɪk/` | headache; my head aches | I have a headache today. | 疼痛 | 日常 |
| recover | v. `/rɪˈkʌvɚ/` | recover from an illness | She is recovering from the flu. | 康复 | 普通 |
| rest | n./v. `/rɛst/` | get some rest | You should get some rest. | 休息 | 日常 |
| medication | n. `/ˌmɛdɪˈkeɪʃən/` | take medication | Are you taking any medication? | 药物 | 普通 |
| appointment | n. `/əˈpɔɪntmənt/` | make a medical appointment | I need to make an appointment. | 预约 | 普通 |
| urgent | adj. `/ˈɝdʒənt/` | urgent care; urgent problem | This may need urgent care. | 紧急的 | 普通 |
| emergency | n. `/ɪˈmɝdʒənsi/` | call emergency services | Call emergency services in an emergency. | 紧急情况 | 正式 |

描述症状时说 `I have a headache.`、`My back hurts.`、`I feel dizzy.`。不要说 `I am headache.`。涉及严重、突发或危及安全的情况，应及时联系当地医疗或紧急服务；本教材不替代专业医疗建议。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| recover | recovery (n.) | `recovery time` 是恢复时间 |
| urgent | urgently (adv.) | `need help urgently` 用于紧急程度 |
| medication | medicate (v.) | 日常沟通优先使用 `medication` 或具体药名 |

`feel sick` 在美式英语中常指恶心或不舒服；`be sick` 可指生病。向医生或服务人员求助时，应使用完整时间信息：`I've had this pain for two days.`

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I am headache.` | `I have a headache.` | `headache` 是可数名词 |
| `My throat is pain.` | `My throat hurts.` 或 `I have a sore throat.` | 不使用 `be pain` |
| `I feel badly.`（表示身体不适） | `I feel bad.` 或 `I feel sick.` | `feel` 后通常接形容词 |
| `I have a fever since yesterday.` | `I've had a fever since yesterday.` | 持续到现在用现在完成时更准确 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择表达

1. I ___ a sore throat. (`have / am`)
2. My shoulder ___. (`hurts / pains`)
3. She is recovering ___ the flu. (`from / of`)

参考答案：1 `have`；2 `hurts`；3 `from`。

### 练习 B：补全求助信息

用 `symptoms / since / appointment` 补全：`I've had these ___ ___ Monday. I need an ___.`

参考答案：`symptoms / since / appointment`。

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：模拟向诊所说明一个非紧急症状，包含部位、感觉、持续时间和已采取的措施。
- 写作：写一条 60-80 词的请假或预约消息，语气礼貌、信息完整，不提供不必要的私人细节。
- 自检：确认症状名词前的冠词、`hurt` 形式及 `for/since` 时间表达。
