# 04-11 词族、词形变化和搭配

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book4  
> 难度：初级至中级  
> 内容类型：词汇构词与搭配

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够根据句子位置选择正确词形，使用高频名词、动词、形容词和副词搭配，并通过上下文猜测词义。

## 2. 核心词族

| 动词 | 名词 | 形容词 | 副词 | 示例搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| decide | decision | decisive | decisively | make a decision |
| communicate | communication | communicative | — | clear communication |
| organize | organization | organized | — | organize a meeting |
| rely | reliability | reliable | reliably | rely on a reliable source |
| respond | response | responsive | responsively | respond to a request |
| succeed | success | successful | successfully | achieve success |
| differ | difference | different | differently | a significant difference |
| create | creation | creative | creatively | create a solution |

英语词缀有规律但不是绝对规则。`-tion` 常构成名词，`-able` 常构成形容词，`-ly` 常构成副词，但要同时记住词的重音、拼写和搭配。

## 3. 搭配记忆法

不要只记单个词，至少记录“词 + 常见对象/介词”：`rely on information`、`respond to a request`、`be responsible for a task`。同一个词族在句子中的位置不同：`a successful project`（形容词），`The project succeeded.`（动词），`The project was completed successfully.`（副词）。

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `We need a reliable information.` | `We need reliable information.` | `information` 通常不可数 |
| `She is responsibility for it.` | `She is responsible for it.` | 需要形容词 `responsible` |
| `He responded my email.` | `He responded to my email.` | `respond` 后接 `to` |
| `The plan worked successful.` | `The plan worked successfully.` | 修饰动词用副词 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择词形

1. We need clear ___. (`communicate / communication`)
2. She is very ___. (`organize / organized`)
3. The team completed the task ___. (`successful / successfully`)
4. Please respond ___ this message. (`to / for`)

参考答案：1 `communication`；2 `organized`；3 `successfully`；4 `to`。

### 练习 B：词族改写

用 `decide` 的适当词形填空：`We need to ___ today. The final ___ is yours.`

参考答案：`decide`；`decision`。

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：用 60 秒说明一个项目或学习计划，至少使用 4 个不同词族成员。
- 写作：写 100-120 词的短说明，标出 8 个词族成员及其句子功能（名词、动词、形容词或副词）。
- 自检：检查词形、介词搭配、可数性和重音；不确定的词查词典例句。
