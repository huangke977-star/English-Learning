## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够描述设备和网络问题，说明基本操作，并用清楚的词汇向客服或同事求助。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| device | n. `/dɪˈvaɪs/` | mobile device; connect a device | This device needs an update. | 设备 | 普通 |
| screen | n. `/skrin/` | screen is frozen/cracked | My screen is frozen. | 屏幕 | 普通 |
| charger | n. `/ˈtʃɑrdʒɚ/` | plug in a charger | I forgot my phone charger. | 充电器 | 日常 |
| battery | n. `/ˈbætəri/` | charge/check the battery | The battery is almost dead. | 电池 | 日常 |
| password | n. `/ˈpæsˌwɝd/` | reset/enter a password | I need to reset my password. | 密码 | 普通 |
| account | n. `/əˈkaʊnt/` | create/access an account | I can't access my account. | 账户 | 普通 |
| log in | v. phr. `/lɔɡ ɪn/` | log in to a site | Please log in to the portal. | 登录 | 普通 |
| upload | v. `/ʌpˈloʊd/` | upload a file/photo | Upload the file before noon. | 上传 | 普通 |
| download | v. `/ˈdaʊnˌloʊd/` | download an app/file | I downloaded the latest version. | 下载 | 普通 |
| update | n./v. `/ʌpˈdeɪt/` | install an update | The app needs an update. | 更新 | 普通 |

`log in` 表示登录，`sign up` 表示注册；`log in to` 后接网站或账户。说明技术问题时按“设备 + 现象 + 已尝试步骤”组织：`My laptop won't connect to Wi-Fi. I restarted it, but the problem remains.`

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| connect | connection (n.), connected (adj.) | `a stable connection` |
| access | accessible (adj.) | `access an account` 不加 `to` 作动词 |
| secure | security (n.), securely (adv.) | 账户安全说明中常见 |

`The battery is dead` 是日常说法，意思是电量耗尽；`The device is powered off` 更中性、偏书面。不要在公开消息中发送真实密码或验证码。

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I can't login my account.` | `I can't log in to my account.` | 动词短语分写并接 `to` |
| `Please access to the page.` | `Please access the page.` | `access` 作动词不接 `to` |
| `I uploaded to the file.` | `I uploaded the file.` | `upload` 直接接宾语 |
| `The Wi-Fi doesn't connect.` | `My laptop won't connect to Wi-Fi.` | 说明设备连接更自然 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择短语

1. I can't ___ to the portal. (`log in / sign up`)
2. Please ___ the form as a PDF. (`upload / download`)
3. I need to ___ my password. (`reset / restart`)

参考答案：1 `log in`；2 `upload`；3 `reset`。

### 练习 B：问题报告

用 `screen / restarted / still` 完成：`My ___ is frozen. I ___ the device, but it is ___ frozen.`

参考答案：`screen / restarted / still`。

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：向客服说明一个设备问题，包含设备、现象、时间和已尝试的解决方法。
- 写作：写一条 80-100 词的技术支持工单，不写真实密码，提出一个明确请求。
- 自检：检查 `log in to`、`access`、`upload/download` 和问题描述顺序。
