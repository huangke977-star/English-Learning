# 04-05 工作、任务和会议

> 状态：已完成（审查与复审通过）  
> 所属册：Book4  
> 难度：初级  
> 内容类型：主题词汇、搭配与输出

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明工作任务和进度，参加基础会议交流，并用清楚、专业而不过分正式的词汇提出跟进。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| task | n. `/tæsk/` | complete/assign a task | I completed the first task. | 任务 | 普通 |
| deadline | n. `/ˈdɛdˌlaɪn/` | meet/miss a deadline | The deadline is Friday afternoon. | 截止时间 | 普通 |
| priority | n. `/praɪˈɔrəti/` | top/high priority | This is our top priority. | 优先事项 | 普通 |
| progress | n. `/ˈprɑɡrɛs/` | make/report progress | We have made good progress. | 进展 | 普通 |
| update | n./v. `/ʌpˈdeɪt/` | provide an update | Could you provide an update? | 更新；进展说明 | 普通 |
| agenda | n. `/əˈdʒɛndə/` | meeting agenda; add to the agenda | I added it to the agenda. | 议程 | 普通 |
| follow up | v. phr. `/ˈfɑloʊ ʌp/` | follow up on/with | I'll follow up with the client. | 跟进 | 普通 |
| coordinate | v. `/koʊˈɔrdəneɪt/` | coordinate with a team | We need to coordinate with design. | 协调 | 普通 |
| approve | v. `/əˈpruːv/` | approve a request | The manager approved the request. | 批准 | 普通 |
| postpone | v. `/poʊˈspoʊn/` | postpone a meeting | We postponed the meeting until Monday. | 推迟 | 普通 |

`make progress` 是固定搭配，通常不说 `do progress`。`follow up on` 接事项，`follow up with` 接人：`follow up on the request`，`follow up with Sam`。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| approve | approval (n.), approved (adj.) | `get approval for` 表示获得批准 |
| coordinate | coordination (n.) | 名词常见于普通和正式书面语 |
| postpone | postponed (adj.) | `The meeting has been postponed.` 常见于通知 |

对同事可说 `Any updates?`，简洁但适合熟悉的工作关系；在邮件或较正式场景用 `Could you provide an update on ...?`

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I have many works.` | `I have a lot of work.` 或 `I have many tasks.` | `work` 通常不可数 |
| `We will discuss about it.` | `We will discuss it.` | `discuss` 后不加 `about` |
| `Please follow the client.` | `Please follow up with the client.` | `follow` 与 `follow up` 含义不同 |
| `The meeting postponed.` | `The meeting was postponed.` | 被动句需要 `be + 过去分词` |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：选择搭配

1. We need to ___ the deadline. (`meet / do`)
2. Please ___ an update by noon. (`provide / make`)
3. I will follow up ___ the request tomorrow. (`on / with`)

参考答案：1 `meet`；2 `provide`；3 `on`。

### 练习 B：语体改写

把 `Any updates?` 改为适合工作邮件的句子。

参考答案：`Could you provide an update on this task?`

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：做一次 45 秒工作进度汇报，说明任务、进展、风险和下一步。
- 写作：写一封 90-120 词的跟进邮件，包含议程或任务、截止时间、需要对方确认的内容和礼貌结尾。
- 自检：检查 `work/tasks`、`discuss`、`follow up on/with` 和被动通知结构。
