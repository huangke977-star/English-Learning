# 04-06 学习、计划和目标

## 1. 学习目标

完成本单元后，你能够说明学习计划、设定可执行目标、表达困难与调整，并用目标词汇完成一段学习反思。

## 2. 核心词汇与搭配

| 词语 | 词性与 IPA | 常用搭配 | 例句 | 中文 | 语体 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| goal | n. `/ɡoʊl/` | set/achieve a goal | I set a weekly learning goal. | 目标 | 普通 |
| focus | n./v. `/ˈfoʊkəs/` | focus on; stay focused | Try to focus on one skill. | 专注；重点 | 普通 |
| habit | n. `/ˈhæbɪt/` | build/break a habit | Reading daily builds a habit. | 习惯 | 普通 |
| improve | v. `/ɪmˈpruːv/` | improve a skill; improve at | I want to improve my writing. | 改进；提高 | 普通 |
| review | n./v. `/rɪˈvjuː/` | review notes; a quick review | Review your notes every Friday. | 复习；审查 | 普通 |
| practice | n./v. `/ˈpræktɪs/` | practice speaking; regular practice | Regular practice matters. | 练习 | 普通 |
| challenge | n./v. `/ˈtʃælɪndʒ/` | face a challenge | Listening is still a challenge. | 挑战 | 普通 |
| strategy | n. `/ˈstrætədʒi/` | learning strategy | This strategy saves time. | 策略 | 普通 |
| achieve | v. `/əˈtʃiːv/` | achieve a goal/result | She achieved her goal. | 达成 | 普通 |
| give up | v. phr. `/ɡɪv ʌp/` | give up doing something | Don't give up practicing. | 放弃 | 日常 |

`practice` 在美式英语中名词和动词都拼作 `practice`。常说 `practice speaking`，不说 `practice to speak`。`improve` 可接宾语，也可用 `improve at + 技能`：`improve at listening`。

## 3. 词形与语体

| 基本词 | 常见变化 | 使用提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| achieve | achievement (n.) | `a sense of achievement` 是成就感 |
| improve | improvement (n.) | `make an improvement` 比 `make progress` 更具体 |
| strategy | strategic (adj.) | `strategic plan` 偏正式 |

`goal` 是可量化或明确的目标，`plan` 是达到目标的安排。写计划时优先说清频率、时长和完成条件，而不只说 `I want to get better.`

## 4. 常见错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I want improve my English.` | `I want to improve my English.` | `want` 后接不定式 |
| `I practice to speak every day.` | `I practice speaking every day.` | `practice` 后接动名词 |
| `I have a plan to my goal.` | `I have a plan to achieve my goal.` | 需要表达动作 |
| `I gave up to learn English.` | `I gave up learning English.` | `give up` 后接动名词 |

## 5. 受控练习

### 练习 A：完成搭配

1. set a weekly ___
2. focus ___ pronunciation
3. give up ___ excuses

参考答案：1 `goal`；2 `on`；3 `making`。

### 练习 B：改写为可执行目标

把 `I want to improve English.` 改为包含频率和具体技能的目标句。

参考答案示例：`I will practice listening for 20 minutes five days a week.`

## 6. 输出任务

- 口语：说明未来 7 天的一项学习目标、两项行动和一个可能的困难。
- 写作：写一段 90-110 词的学习计划，说明目标、策略、复习日期和如何衡量进步。
- 自检：检查 `want to`、`practice + -ing`、`give up + -ing` 和每个目标是否可衡量。
